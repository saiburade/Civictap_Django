About Karad

Discover the city that stands at the confluence of two rivers.

Karad is a city and municipal council in Satara district of Maharashtra, India. It is situated in the western part of Maharashtra and has an important place in the region's history, agriculture and culture.

One of Karad's most distinctive geographical features is the meeting of the Krishna and Koyna rivers. The confluence is popularly known as the Preeti Sangam and is one of the city's best-known landmarks.

Surrounded by the Sahyadri region and fertile agricultural land, Karad has developed as an important town connecting surrounding villages and communities.

A Glimpse Into Karad's History

A city shaped by centuries of regional history.

🏛️ An Ancient Settlement

Karad has a long history and its location near the confluence of the Krishna and Koyna rivers contributed to its importance as a settlement.

The region has been associated with different dynasties and kingdoms that ruled parts of Maharashtra over the centuries.

⚔️ The Maratha Era

Karad and the surrounding Satara region became significant during the period of Maratha rule.

The wider region played an important role in the political and military history of Maharashtra, particularly during the rise and expansion of Maratha power.

📜 Karad Through Modern History

During the colonial period, Karad continued to develop as an important settlement in the region.

Over time, the town developed stronger connections with education, agriculture, trade and surrounding communities.

🌆 Karad Today

Modern Karad combines its historical identity with education, business, agriculture and civic development.

Its location and connectivity have helped it grow into an important urban centre in southern Maharashtra.
